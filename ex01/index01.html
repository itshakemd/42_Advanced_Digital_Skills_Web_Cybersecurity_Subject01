Why I Am Learning to Code

I am learning to code because I want to understand how digital tools work and build useful things of my own.

Turning ideas into projects

Learning by building

Programming gives me a way to turn an idea into something people can use. Each small project helps me practice problem-solving and become more independent.

Understanding the fundamentals matters because good habits make larger projects easier to build and maintain.

Growing with a community

Sharing and improving

I also want to contribute to open-source projects, learn from other developers, and share what I discover.
Every bug I investigate is another chance to improve.

I do not need to know everything today; I need to keep learning and practicing.
