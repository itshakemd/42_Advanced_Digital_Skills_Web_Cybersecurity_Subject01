Building the Web, One Layer at a Time

Every web page begins with structure. HTML gives content meaning, from headings and paragraphs to navigation and articles.

Starting with semantic HTML makes a page easier to understand for readers, browsers, assistive technology, and search engines. Clear structure is part of good engineering.

My approach is to learn one idea, use it in a small project, and then review what can be improved. Consistency matters more than rushing.

Quick Note

Use an element for the meaning of its content. Keep visual styling separate from the document structure.
